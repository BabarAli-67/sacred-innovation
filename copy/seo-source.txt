What to Expect When You Request an Estimate 
A useful estimate starts with clear information. Contact Sacred Innovations before selecting 
every finish or preparing a complete design. 
1.​ Share the basics. Provide your property ZIP code, the rooms involved, a short task list, 
and photos showing the space. 
2.​ Discuss the scope. Explain what you want to change, what should stay, and any known 
damage, access limits, or earlier repairs. 
3.​ Review the estimate. Ask what work, materials, preparation, disposal, and finishing are 
included. Confirm exclusions, allowances, and how changes are handled. 
4.​ Discuss timing and preparation. Review material availability, access, household 
arrangements, and which areas may be unavailable during the work.

What Affects the Cost of a Home Renovation? 
Project cost depends on more than room size. The work requested, material choices, existing 
conditions, removal, preparation, access, and layout changes all affect the estimate. Plumbing, 
electrical, or structural work may also require specialist trades or additional approvals. 
To make your inquiry more useful, prepare: 
●​ Your property ZIP code and the rooms involved. 
●​ Photos of the full room and areas needing attention. 
●​ Approximate measurements, if available. 
●​ Known damage, earlier repairs, or existing materials. 
●​ The changes you want and features you plan to keep. 
●​ Product links, preferred finishes, or inspiration images. 
●​ Your preferred timing and budget range, if known. 
●​ The best way and time to reach you for follow-up. 
A clear first message helps us understand the request and respond with relevant next steps. If 
you have a target start date, mention it early so scheduling can be discussed before you make 
plans around the work. 
When comparing renovation estimates, check that each proposal covers similar work. Review 
demolition, disposal, surface preparation, materials, installation, finishing, cleanup, exclusions, 
and allowances. A lower total may include fewer tasks, so compare the written scope rather 
than the headline price alone. Read how to compare remodeling estimates line by line.
